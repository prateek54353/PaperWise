Powerful Features

Everything you need to digitize, organize, and share your documents

📷

Smart Document Scanning

Capture documents directly using your camera or import multiple images at once from your gallery. Advanced edge detection and auto-enhancement ensure crisp, clear scans every time.

  • Auto-edge detection
  • Batch image import
  • Auto-enhancement filters
✏️

Advanced Editing Tools

Reorder, crop, rotate, and apply filters to your pages before exporting. Complete undo/redo history controls let you experiment freely without losing your work.

  • Crop & rotate pages
  • Color filters & adjustments
  • Full undo/redo history
📄

Professional PDF Creation

Create high-quality PDFs with customizable page sizes (A4, Letter, Legal, or Fit to Image). Adjust compression levels to balance quality and file size for your specific needs.

  • Multiple page sizes
  • Adjustable compression
  • High-quality output
🔀

PDF Merge & Split

Merge multiple PDFs with intuitive drag-to-reorder interface and preview thumbnails. Split PDFs from a visual page selector - extract specific pages or export all pages separately.

  • Drag-to-reorder merging
  • Visual page selector
  • Batch export options
📊

Smart Document Management

Organize your PDFs with powerful sorting options by date, name, size, or page count. Quick actions let you merge, share, or delete documents with long-press convenience.

  • Multiple sort options
  • Quick actions menu
  • Bulk operations
🎨

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about PaperWise

Is PaperWise really free?

+

Yes! PaperWise is completely free and open source. There are no premium features, subscriptions, or hidden costs. The app is funded entirely through voluntary user support.

Does PaperWise require internet connection?

+

No, PaperWise works completely offline. All document processing, PDF creation, and file management happen locally on your device. Internet is only needed for downloading updates or accessing external links.

What permissions does PaperWise need?

+

PaperWise only requests essential permissions: Camera (for scanning), Storage (for saving documents), and Internet (for update checks). No location, contacts, or other invasive permissions are required.

Can I use PaperWise for commercial purposes?

+

Absolutely! PaperWise is released under the MIT License, which allows for both personal and commercial use. You can use it for business documents, client work, or any commercial application without restrictions.

How do I get updates for PaperWise?

+

For automatic updates, install PaperWise through IzzyOnDroid F-Droid repository. If you downloaded from GitHub, you'll need to manually check for new releases and download the updated APK.

Is my data safe with PaperWise?

+

Your data is completely safe with PaperWise. All files are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded to external servers unless you explicitly share them. The app is open source, so you can verify the code yourself.
