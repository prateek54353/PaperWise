🔒 Your Privacy Matters

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2024

PaperWise is built with privacy as a fundamental principle. This document explains how we handle your data and protect your information.

1. Introduction

PaperWise is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use our document scanner and PDF toolkit application.

PaperWise is designed to be private by default. We believe that your documents and personal information should remain on your device unless you explicitly choose to share them.

2. Data Collection

2.1 Information We Collect

PaperWise collects minimal information necessary for the app to function:

  • Document Files: Images and PDFs that you create or import are stored locally on your device
  • App Settings: Your preferences for themes, compression settings, and other app configurations
  • Device Information: Basic device information for compatibility and performance optimization

2.2 Information We Don't Collect

PaperWise does NOT collect:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or tracking data
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Document content for analysis or processing

3. Data Storage and Processing

3.1 Local Storage

All documents and files you create or import into PaperWise are stored locally on your device. We do not upload your documents to any external servers unless you explicitly choose to share them through the app's sharing features.

3.2 No Cloud Services

PaperWise does not use cloud services for document storage, processing, or backup. Your documents remain under your control at all times.

3.3 File Sharing

When you choose to share a document through PaperWise (via email, messaging apps, or other methods), the document is shared only through the mechanisms you select. We do not intercept or store shared documents.

4. Permissions

PaperWise requests only the minimum permissions necessary for core functionality:

  • Camera: Required to capture document photos
  • Storage: Required to save and access documents on your device
  • Internet: Required only for checking app updates and linking to external resources

We do not request permissions for advertising, tracking, or data collection purposes.

5. No Advertising or Tracking

PaperWise is completely free from:

  • Third-party advertising
  • Analytics and tracking services
  • User behavior monitoring
  • Data selling or sharing with third parties

We generate revenue through voluntary user support (coffee donations) rather than advertising or data monetization.

6. Open Source Transparency

PaperWise is open source software released under the MIT License. This means:

  • The complete source code is publicly available on GitHub
  • Anyone can review the code to verify our privacy practices
  • The community can contribute to security audits and improvements
  • There are no hidden features or data collection mechanisms

You can review our source code at: https://github.com/prateek54353/PaperWise

7. Data Security

While PaperWise stores data locally on your device, we recommend:

  • Keeping your device secure with a lock screen password or biometric authentication
  • Regularly backing up important documents through your device's backup system
  • Being cautious when sharing documents through third-party apps

PaperWise does not provide additional encryption beyond what your device's operating system offers.

8. Children's Privacy

PaperWise is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. As the app does not collect personal information, this is not applicable to our service.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

Significant changes will be highlighted in the app's release notes on GitHub.

10. Contact and Questions

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or PaperWise's privacy practices, you can:

  • Open an issue on our GitHub repository: https://github.com/prateek54353/PaperWise/issues
  • Review the source code to verify our practices
  • Contribute to the project's development

11. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the jurisdiction in which you use PaperWise. By using the app, you agree to this Privacy Policy.
